Week-by-Week Guide: 2D vs. 4D

2D ultrasound is used for medical screening — checking your baby's growth, organs, and measurements. 4D ultrasound captures lifelike, three-dimensional facial expressions and movements that help you bond with your baby before birth. Enter your dates above and the matching row below will highlight.

Week What 2D Shows What 4D Shows 4D Clarity
Ultrasound Terms & Abbreviations
GSGestational SacThe sac that holds your baby early in pregnancy.
CRLCrown-Rump LengthHead-to-bottom measurement used to confirm your due date.
BPDBiparietal DiameterThe widest measurement across your baby's head.
ACAbdominal CircumferenceMeasurement around the belly, used to track growth.
FLFemur LengthLength of the thigh bone, used to track bone growth.
EFWEstimated Fetal WeightEstimated weight, calculated from BPD, AC, and FL.
GAGestational AgeYour baby's estimated age, based on measurements.
EDDEstimated Date of DeliveryYour baby's due date.
FHRFetal Heart RateYour baby's heartbeat, in beats per minute.
AFIAmniotic Fluid IndexA measurement of your amniotic fluid level.
