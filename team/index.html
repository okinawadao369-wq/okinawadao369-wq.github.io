Our Team

Meet Our Team

A small, dedicated team built to make pregnancy and family care feel personal, not clinical.

🌿

Koya Miyagi

Founder & Director
  • Founder, 合同会社koya

Born and raised in Okinawa, our founder started Family Tree Oki to give military and SOFA-status families a warm, English-speaking place to experience pregnancy milestones close to base — without the language barrier or guesswork that so often comes with navigating care overseas.

🌿

Our Ultrasound Team

Sonography & Imaging
  • English-speaking
  • All-female team

Our all-female ultrasound team specializes in 4D, 2D, and gender-confirmation imaging for expecting parents. Every session is explained clearly in English, with time for your questions and your family.

🌿

Fumiya Shimabukuro

Licensed Acupuncturist & Moxibustionist
  • はり師・きゅう師 National License, Japan

Fumiya Shimabukuro is a nationally licensed acupuncturist and moxibustionist (はり師・きゅう師), born in Okinawa. He now offers appointment-based acupuncture sessions inside the Family Tree Oki studio.

🌿

Shiori

Guest Services & Interpretation
  • Bilingual Guest Support (English/Japanese)

Shiori supports English-speaking families from their very first message through checkout, interpreting between English and Japanese so nothing gets lost along the way. She loves connecting with guests and making sure every family feels comfortable, informed, and well cared for during their visit.

🌿

Atsuko

Midwife
  • Licensed Midwife (助産師), Japan

Atsuko is a licensed midwife who helps guide the moment your baby's face first appears on screen. She takes care to make each 4D ultrasound feel calm and reassuring, so families can simply relax and enjoy this once-in-a-lifetime moment of meeting their baby before birth.

🌿

Nobu

Registered Nurse, Obstetrics & Gynecology
  • 30+ Years OB/GYN Nursing Experience

Nobu is a registered nurse with more than 30 years of experience in obstetrics and gynecology. A lifelong lover of babies, she says every session leaves her feeling grateful for the chance to share in a family's most emotional moments.
